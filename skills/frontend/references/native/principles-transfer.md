# Principles Transfer: Keep, Adapt, Exclude

This is a scoped interpretation of the existing design philosophy, not a replacement of its source packages. **KEEP** preserves a transferable principle; **ADAPT** changes its expression for native interactive products; **EXCLUDE** removes a source-specific prescription from this skill, not the technique from all possible design work. Source paths below resolve through installed skills. Baseline mechanics and Apple/Android framework advice in the platform references are native applications of these principles, not claims that the source text or search database contains every detail.

For Android, **KEEP** product intent, hierarchy, rhythm, brand expression and explicit states. **ADAPT** semantic roles to existing Android themes and resources, layout to dp and text to sp, accessible touch regions to at least 48×48dp, and interaction to system insets, Back and TalkBack. A touch region is not a compulsory icon or visual-control size. See [Android native application](android-native.md) and its official Android sources for platform-specific design, implementation and verification; [density guidance](https://developer.android.com/training/multiscreen/screendensities) and [accessibility guidance](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/apps) ground the units and touch targets here, not the older source packages below. **EXCLUDE** importing Apple pt, SF Symbols or iOS navigation as Android defaults, web units/toolchains, and mandatory dynamic color, Material or Compose migration. Preserve the app's supported theme and toolkit, including Kotlin/Java Views and mixed hosting.

| `Output Format`; `Use Cases` | **EXCLUDE** mandatory philosophy `.md` plus PDF/PNG outputs and poster/editorial/exhibition deliverable constraints. **KEEP** conceptual coherence where useful; native source and exercised app behavior are the relevant deliverables. |

The inherited philosophy therefore remains broad: distinctive expression, meaningful space and form, hierarchy, rhythm, proportion, cohesive color and typography, systematic variation, and repeated visual refinement. The exclusions remove medium-specific prescriptions and ceremony—not density, custom type, animation, materials, playful character or ambition.
